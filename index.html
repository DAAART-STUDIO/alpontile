A place shaped by history

Dining beside the Castello Scaligero

At Ristorante al Pontile, the lake meets the history of Malcesine. Just beyond the water stands the Castello Scaligero, the town's iconic fortress rebuilt by the Scaliger family around 1300. From a table by the lake, guests can experience the landscape that has defined Malcesine for centuries — the Garda shoreline, the medieval castle and the mountains of Monte Baldo.

The Cuisine

Italian cuisine by Lake Garda

At Ristorante al Pontile, Italian tradition meets the flavours of Lake Garda. The menu brings together freshwater fish from the lake, seafood, homemade pasta, selected meats and classic Italian dishes, alongside traditional and gourmet pizzas. From grilled salmon trout and Garda fish pasta to spaghetti allo scoglio, Scottona beef and homemade desserts, the cuisine combines familiar Italian flavours with the character of the lake.
